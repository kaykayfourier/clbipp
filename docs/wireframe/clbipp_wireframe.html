What this document is

Mid-fidelity wireframes mapping every screen, transaction, and state of the Module 2 spec — built so engineering can pick it up and build it, and so the team lead can review the flow before a single component ships.

Every screen ties back to a layer of the engine: intake, damage scoring, BMS gate, SoH eligibility, economics, selection.

Section 02 · Information Architecture

The engine, in six layers.

Every screen in this wireframe is a window onto one of these layers. Layers 0–3 are eligibility filters that narrow the candidate set; Layer 4 runs the economics; Layer 5 picks a winner and produces a price band. The flow is the same whether the battery comes from internal warehouse stock or an external supplier — only the output framing differs.

Section 03 · Two interfaces, one engine

Field-facing speed.
Floor-facing depth.

A Field Agent App · mobile

Inspectors at supplier sites & warehouse intake. Speed, clarity, no ambiguity.
  • Login & assigned pickups
  • QR scan / manual battery ID
  • Battery intake form (chemistry, weight, capacity)
  • Damage assessment — 3 weighted tests with rubric
  • BMS sync & SoH read confirmation
  • Quote computing (transparent loading)
  • Decision result — pathway, price band, breakdown
  • Negotiate & confirm purchase
  • Pickup scheduling & logistics
  • Battery tracking (lifecycle timeline)
  • History · my quotes · my pickups
  • Profile & settings

B Internal Dashboard · desktop

Ops & commercial team. Margins, exceptions, configuration, audit.
  • Overview — KPIs, throughput, margin health
  • Live quote queue (in-flight decisions)
  • Battery detail — full audit trail per trace_id
  • Inventory — warehouse-level view
  • Engine config — margin tiers & rate cards (versioned)
  • Supplier management & per-account margin overrides
  • Market data — live LME & Fast Markets feed
  • Exception queue — HOLD / REVIEW items
  • Traceability deep-view — full lifecycle map
  • Compliance reports — CPCB / EPR exports
Design principle Eligibility filters; economics selects. The Field Agent app surfaces the engine's output — pathway and price — never its internals. The Internal Dashboard surfaces the engine's state — every input, override, and rate that produced that decision, immutably logged.

Section 06 · Field Agent App · Assessment

Layer 1 visual. Layer 2 invisible.

Damage scoring is the inspector's job — three weighted tests against established standards (Suzuki IDIS, DOT 188, EUCAR), each scored 0–3 with photo evidence. The BMS gate is the engine's job — anomalies that aren't visible to the eye. The agent confirms what they can see; the engine handles the rest.

Section 09 · Field Agent App · Lifecycle Tracking

One battery, one timeline.

The traceability view is the answer to "where is my battery now?". Each trace_id moves through a deterministic sequence of states — Collected → In Transit → At Warehouse → Tested → Processed → Certified — with timestamps, location codes, and operator IDs at every hop. The supplier sees the same view via their tracking QR. EPR-grade audit trail; no black box.
